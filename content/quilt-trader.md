Trading research you can trace. A distributed framework for collecting market data, testing strategies, and running algorithms on your own machines.

Quilt Trader keeps market data in a local Parquet store and exposes the research loop through an agent-friendly CLI. Its options backtests model trade direction and execution assumptions, with an adjustable realism setting.

A coordinator, dashboard, and distributed workers connect research to execution. Currently in private alpha, with an open-source release planned.
