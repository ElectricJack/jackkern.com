# Garden and architecture pass — 2026-09-16

[Local build](http://localhost:4321/) · [Entry courtyard](views/desktop-entry-view.png) · [Mobile entry](views/mobile-entry-view.png)

The existing Matter kit now has a richer architectural and planted setting. The continuous camera route and its speed profile remain unchanged.

## Architecture and planting

- Arched marble surrounds frame all eight doorways, with individual voussoirs, recessed mortar and stone jambs. Three courses of rusticated masonry and a marble dado wrap the room walls.
- A broad three-run arrival stair connects the raised entry to the garden. Marble posts and bronze handrails finish the arrival stairs and the descending passage into Quilt Trader.
- Boundary columns whose capitals/bases penetrated neighboring walls have been removed; duplicate columns and beams at shared joins are removed too. Walls carry the cornice at those joins. Actual exported bounds are checked for column/wall intersections.
- Seven additional olive planters bring the total to seventeen. Procedural ferns fill their bases, garden beds edge the open colonnades, vines hang from cornices, and small climbers wind around selected columns. Opaque geometric leaves avoid alpha-texture sorting.
- Carved wall panels now face both sides of end walls, giving the approaches and departures more detail.

There are still **21 unique Matter assets**, now arranged into **457 placements**. Additional architecture and plants use shared procedural geometry and instanced batches. The complete garden layer has 7,020 leaves on desktop or 4,272 on mobile; only the resident stops are shown. These are small leaf meshes, not extra downloaded models. Native Matter sources and master exports are unchanged.

## Marble, water and atmosphere

Fine marble veins are generated in world space, with derivative filtering to soften subpixel lines. Marble finishes extend across columns, cornices, coping, stairs, benches, paving, selected statuary and planters. Bronze, ceramics and soil retain their material identities. No new bitmap texture maps are downloaded.

Water combines several wave directions, small fountain impact rings, a restrained bottom-tile/caustic pattern, Fresnel reflection and sunlight glints. Falling fountain streams are instanced. One shared planar reflection captures nearby geometry at **512² on desktop or 256² on mobile**, with no multisampling. The pass refreshes on camera movement or scene changes, at most approximately 30/15 times per second while moving. A paused camera reuses its reflection while the ripple shader continues. Pools at the active water height share the reflection; elevated fountain bowls use a sky-color approximation. This is not full scene refraction or a fluid simulation.

Sparse dust particles drift through the space and sample the sun's shadow map, dimming in shade. Slight exponential haze softens distant geometry. There are 650 dust points on desktop or 280 on mobile. Water and dust continue gently with the camera paused; reduced-motion mode freezes them, and hidden tabs stop updating the scene. Switching to the reading page also stops drawing the hidden canvas.

The graphics use runtime Three.js lighting and shadows. This pass does not introduce baked world illumination.

## Delivery and verification

The existing complete GLB deliveries remain **2.036 MB desktop / 1.684 MB mobile** before HTTP compression. New detail is generated by code. Fully replaced marble materials release their old atlases: estimated complete-kit texture memory falls from **119.4 to 71.1 MiB desktop**, and **35.6 to 23.5 MiB mobile**. This estimate excludes geometry, reflection/shadow buffers, other render targets and driver overhead. The discarded atlases are still inside the existing GLBs, so this is a GPU memory saving rather than a file-size reduction.
